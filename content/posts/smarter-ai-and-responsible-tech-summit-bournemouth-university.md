♻️✨️ A really enjoyable guest lecture with the Communications and Marketing Master’s students at Bournemouth University earlier today. We talked about sustainability, responsible business, digital waste and zero waste, including looking at the less obvious relationship between waste and the way we communicate digitally.

💡 I went in to share some of my experiences, but the session quickly became a two-way conversation, with some great questions, different perspectives and ideas coming from the students. I probably came away thinking about a few things differently myself, which is always the sign of a good session.
